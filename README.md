# 600 Stockton Street

A digital heritage research project exploring the architectural,
cultural, and social history of 600 Stockton Street in San Francisco.

## Research Questions

- What stood at this site before 600 Stockton?
- Who designed the buildings?
- How did the architecture change over time?
- What stories are connected to the site?
- How did the surrounding Nob Hill and Chinatown neighborhoods evolve?
- How can digital technology make this history accessible to visitors?

## Project Goal

To create an open-source, evidence-based digital experience
that allows people to explore the history of 600 Stockton Street
through maps, photographs, architectural research, timelines,
and eventually interactive/immersive technologies.# 600-stockton-README.md
