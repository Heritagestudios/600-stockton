# 600 Stockton Street — Architectural Phase Evidence

## Purpose

This file connects each major architectural phase of 600 Stockton Street
to the people, sources, photographs, maps, and physical evidence associated
with that phase.

The goal is to distinguish documented architectural history from later
interpretation.

---

# Phase 1 — Original Metropolitan Life Headquarters

## Date

1908–1909

## Building

Metropolitan Life Insurance Company Pacific Coast Head Office

## Primary Architectural Association

Napoleon LeBrun & Sons

## Local Architectural Association

James Rupert Miller

## Architectural Character

The original building established the monumental classical identity that
would define the later complex.

The research describes:

- classical facade composition
- Ionic architectural elements
- monumental entrance
- steel-frame construction
- terra-cotta exterior

The original building occupied only part of the eventual complex.

## Evidence

### Architectural documentation

PCAD and historic architectural research.

### Cartographic evidence

Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps.

### Photographic evidence

Historic photographs of the early Metropolitan Life building.

### Current status

**Documented in broad form.**

Exact original floor-plan geometry remains a research question.

# Architectural Participants

## Napoleon LeBrun & Sons

**Role:** Original architectural design.

**Phase:** 1908–1909.

**Research significance:**

Established the original classical architectural language of the
Metropolitan Life headquarters.

---

## James Rupert Miller

**Role:** Local architect and major participant in subsequent expansions.

**Phases:**

- 1908–1909
- 1913–1914
- 1919–1920
- later expansion work

**Research significance:**

Miller provides an important continuity link between the original building
and later expansion campaigns.

---

## Timothy L. Pflueger

**Role:** Designer associated with later Metropolitan Life expansions.

**Phases:**

- 1913–1914
- 1919–1920
- later improvements

**Research significance:**

The Metropolitan Life additions form an important early chapter in
Pflueger's career.

---

## Haig Patigian

**Role:** Sculptor.

**Associated work:**

Monumental architectural sculpture and decorative work.

**Research significance:**

Francisco's broader history of architectural sculpture.

---

## Gladding, McBean & Company

**Role:** Architectural terra-cotta manufacturer.

**Associated periods:**

Multiple expansion campaigns.

**Known archival leads:**

- 1919 Metropolitan Life addition
- 1921 Sun Room project

of materials, fabrication, and installation.

# Evidence Classification

Each future source should be assigned one of these categories:

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PRIMARY | Original map, photograph, drawing, permit, contract, correspondence |
| SECONDARY | Scholarly or institutional interpretation |
| TERTIARY | General web source or unspecialized summary |
| INFERRED | Historical interpretation based on multiple sources |
| UNKNOWN | Information requiring verification |

# Evidence-Control Principle

No architectural phase should be represented as a precise reconstruction
until its geometry is supported by primary evidence.

The project will distinguish:

**WHAT WE KNOW**

from

**WHAT WE INFER**

from

**WHAT WE DO NOT YET KNOW**

This distinction is fundamental to the credibility of the digital
reconstruction.
