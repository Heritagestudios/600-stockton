# Modeling Rules

## Rule 1 — Map First

The 1919 Sanborn footprint is the primary source for the horizontal
building geometry.

---

## Rule 2 — Photographs Second

Historic photographs may establish:

- facade appearance
- architectural details
- apparent height
- roofline
- entrances
- streetscape

They should not override the Sanborn footprint without an explanation.

---

## Rule 3 — No Silent Reconstruction

If an architectural feature cannot be established from available evidence,
it must be labeled:

**RECONSTRUCTED**

or

**UNKNOWN**

---

# Confidence Levels

## DOCUMENTED

Directly visible or stated in a primary source.

## SUPPORTED

Supported by multiple independent sources.

## RECONSTRUCTED

Historically informed interpretation based on available evidence.

## UNKNOWN

Insufficient evidence.
