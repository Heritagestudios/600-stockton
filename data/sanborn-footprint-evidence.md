# 600 Stockton Street — Sanborn Footprint Evidence

## Purpose

This file records building-footprint evidence extracted directly from
the Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps for the Metropolitan Life Insurance
Company building at 600 Stockton Street.

The Sanborn maps are treated as primary-source spatial evidence.

---

# 1913–1915 Sanborn Map

## Property Identification

**Block:** 257

**Building:** Metropolitan Life Insurance Co's Bldg.

**Location:** East side of Stockton Street between California and Pine
Streets.

## Original Building Footprint

The Sanborn map depicts the Metropolitan Life building as a rectangular
fireproof structure at the south end of Block 257.

### Dimensions shown on map

**Stockton Street frontage:** approximately 60 feet

**Depth:** approximately 80 feet

### Construction

The map identifies the structure as:

- Fireproof construction
- Steel frame
- Brick and tile exterior walls
