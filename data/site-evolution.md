# 600 Stockton — Site Evolution

## Purpose

This document tracks the physical evolution of the site using
historical Sanborn maps and other primary sources.

The project distinguishes between direct observations,
interpretations, and externally verified historical information.

- **QUESTION** — unresolved research question
- **CONTRADICTION** — conflicting sources
