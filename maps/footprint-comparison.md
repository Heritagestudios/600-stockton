# 600 Stockton Street — Building Footprint Comparison

## Purpose

This file compares the physical evolution of the 600 Stockton Street site
using historic Sanborn Fire Insurance Maps, photographs, and architectural
documentation.

The goal is to move from a narrative history toward an evidence-based
digital reconstruction of the building over time.

---

# 1886–1887 — Before Metropolitan Life

## Site Condition

The 1886–1887 Sanborn map establishes the condition of the site before the
Metropolitan Life Insurance Company building was constructed.

This period provides the baseline for understanding what occupied the
site before the 1909 headquarters.
