# Sanborn Map — 1886–1887

## Map Record

**Map:** Sanborn Fire Insurance Map  
**Date:** 1886–1887  
**Location:** San Francisco, California  
**Relevant area:** California Street / Stockton Street / Pine Street / Grant Avenue  
**Block:** 257  
**Project:** 600 Stockton Street

---

## Why This Map Matters

This map provides a spatial record of the site before the
1906 San Francisco earthquake and fire.

It allows the project to compare the physical landscape before
1906 with later maps showing the Metropolitan Life building.

## Direct Observations from the Map

1. Grace Church is clearly identified on the map.
2. The church occupies a substantial footprint relative to
   neighboring structures.
3. California Street and Stockton Street establish the
   primary spatial relationship for the site.
4. Multiple smaller structures surround the church site.
5. The map provides a pre-1906 baseline against which later
