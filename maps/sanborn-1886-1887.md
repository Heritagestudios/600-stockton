---

## Why This Map Matters

This map provides a spatial record of the site before the
1906 San Francisco earthquake and fire.

It allows the project to compare the physical landscape before
1906 with later maps showing the Metropolitan Life building.

## Questions Raised by the Map

1. What was the exact footprint of Grace Church?
2. What structures occupied the surrounding parcels?
3. Which buildings survived until 1906?
4. Which structures appear in later Sanborn maps?
5. How did the footprint change after 1906?
6. Can the 1886–1887 map be aligned with the 1913–1915 map?
7. Can the historical photographs be matched to specific locations
   shown on the map?
