## Site

### 600 Stockton / Predecessor Site

The 1886–1887 Sanborn map documents the block containing the
site before the construction of the 1909 Metropolitan Life
building.

The map shows **Grace Church** occupying a substantial portion
of the block at the California Street / Stockton Street area.

Building use:

See map notation; requires transcription and verification.

Approximate footprint:

Large masonry structure occupying a significant portion of

## Direct Observations from the Map

1. Grace Church is clearly identified on the map.
2. The church occupies a substantial footprint relative to
   neighboring structures.
3. California Street and Stockton Street establish the
   primary spatial relationship for the site.
4. Multiple smaller structures surround the church site.
5. The map provides a pre-1906 baseline against which later
   Sanborn maps can be compared.## Source Interpretation

### What the Map Directly Shows

- The location of Grace Church.
- The footprint and spatial organization of structures on the block.
- Surrounding streets and neighboring properties.
- Sanborn fire-insurance mapping conventions and building information.

### What Requires Additional Research

- Exact construction date of Grace Church.
- Architect.
- Original architectural drawings.
- Exact dimensions.
- Building materials and structural system.
- Relationship between the mapped footprint and surviving photographs.
- What portions of the structure survived the 1906 earthquake and fire.

### Research Principle

This project distinguishes between information directly visible
in a primary source and information inferred from that source or
obtained from secondary sources.
