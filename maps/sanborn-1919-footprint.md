# 600 Stockton Street — 1919 Sanborn Footprint

## Source

**Map:** Sanborn Fire Insurance Map

**Year:** 1919

**Sheet:** 47

**Subject:** Metropolitan Life Insurance Company

**Site:** 600 Stockton Street, San Francisco

---

# Purpose

This document records the physical footprint evidence visible on the 1919
Sanborn map.

The 1919 map is being used as the primary cartographic source for
reconstructing the expanded Metropolitan Life complex.

The purpose of this record is to distinguish what can be directly observed
from the map from measurements and interpretations that still require
verification.

---

# Building Configuration

The 1919 map documents a substantially expanded Metropolitan Life complex.

The building should be understood as a multi-wing structure rather than
the smaller rectangular building constructed in 1909.

The map indicates an interior open/courtyard condition within the larger
complex.

---

# Footprint Components

## Original 1909 Core

**Status:** To be identified within the 1919 footprint.

The original Metropolitan Life headquarters should be distinguished from
later additions where the evidence allows.

The exact boundary of the 1909 core within the 1919 building remains a
research question.

---

## Later Expansion Areas

**Status:** Documented broadly; individual construction phases require
additional comparison.

The 1919 footprint contains building areas that represent expansion beyond
the original 1909 headquarters.

These areas should eventually be compared against:

- 1913–1915 Sanborn evidence
- historic photographs
- architectural drawings
- permits
- later Sanborn maps

---

## Interior Open / Courtyard Area

**Status:** Visible in the 1919 footprint.

The open area should be digitized as a separate polygon or void rather
than treated as building mass.
