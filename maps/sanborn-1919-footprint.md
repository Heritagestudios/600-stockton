# 600 Stockton Street — 1919 Sanborn Footprint

## Source

**Map:** Sanborn Fire Insurance Map

**Year:** 1919

**Sheet:** 47

**Subject:** Metropolitan Life Insurance Company

**Site:** 600 Stockton Street, San Francisco

---

# Purpose

This document records the physical footprint evidence visible on the 1919
Sanborn map.

The 1919 map is being used as the primary cartographic source for
reconstructing the expanded Metropolitan Life complex.

The purpose of this record is to distinguish what can be directly observed
from the map from measurements and interpretations that still require
verification.

---

# Building Configuration

The 1919 Sanborn map places the Metropolitan Life Insurance Company's
building on the Stockton side of Block 257 (1161).

The map shows a principal rectangular building fronting Stockton Street,
with additional building areas extending northward into the block.

The 1919 configuration should therefore be modeled as a historical
building footprint composed of multiple connected/adjacent areas rather
than as the present-day building.

The map also contains other structures and parcels within Block 257 that
must not be incorporated into the Metropolitan Life footprint.

---

# Footprint Components

## Principal Stockton Building

**Status:** Documented

The principal Metropolitan Life building occupies the Stockton Street
frontage on the southern portion of Block 257.

This is the primary mass to establish in the first 3D reconstruction.

---

## Northern Building Areas

**Status:** Documented broadly

Additional building areas extend northward from the principal Stockton
building.

These areas appear to form connected or closely related portions of the
Metropolitan Life complex.

Their exact geometry should be digitized directly from the Sanborn map
rather than inferred from the present-day building.

---

## Other Structures Within Block 257

The Sanborn sheet contains additional structures within the block that are
not part of the Metropolitan Life building.

These must remain separate from the Metropolitan Life reconstruction unless
additional evidence establishes a connection.

---

# Dimensions

The uploaded Sanborn map contains dimensional annotations, but the
available scan is not sufficiently clear to responsibly transcribe every
dimension without verification.

Therefore, no uncertain dimensions will be entered as fact.

## Required Measurements

Measure directly from the original map:

- Stockton frontage of the Metropolitan Life building
- overall north-south building depth
- width of the principal Stockton building
- width and depth of northern extensions
- offsets between connected building areas
- relationship to the Block 257 property boundaries

---

# Scale

**Status:** Pending verification.

The map should be calibrated using a clearly legible Sanborn scale or
verified dimensional annotation before coordinates are created.

Do not assume a scale from the visual appearance of the scan.
