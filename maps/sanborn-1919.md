## Why This Map Matters

The 1919 Sanborn map provides another snapshot of the 600
Stockton Street site after the construction and early expansion
of the Metropolitan Life Insurance Company's Pacific Coast Head
Office.

It can be compared with the 1886–1887 and 1913–1915 Sanborn maps
to document how the site and surrounding urban landscape changed
over time.
