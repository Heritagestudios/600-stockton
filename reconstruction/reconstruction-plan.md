# 600 Stockton Street — Digital Reconstruction Plan

## Purpose

The digital reconstruction will represent the physical and architectural
evolution of 600 Stockton Street through time.

The project will not begin with detailed architectural modeling.

It will proceed from evidence to geometry to architectural detail to
immersive interpretation.
