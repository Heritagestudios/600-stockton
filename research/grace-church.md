# Grace Church — California & Stockton

## Overview

Grace Church stood at the southeast corner of California and
Stockton Streets on Nob Hill before its destruction in the
1906 San Francisco fire.

## Timeline

### 1849 — Early Grace Chapel

Grace's congregation traces its origins to the Gold Rush era.

### 1862 — Grace Church

A major new church was established at California and Stockton
Streets.

### 1906 — Earthquake and Fire

The April 18, 1906 earthquake struck San Francisco. Grace Church
survived the initial earthquake with relatively limited damage,
but was subsequently destroyed by the fires that swept through
the city.

April 18, 1906 earthquake.
