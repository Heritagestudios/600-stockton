# Grace Church — California & Stockton

## Overview

Grace Church stood at the southeast corner of California and
Stockton Streets before its destruction in the 1906 fire.

## Timeline

### 1849
Grace's congregation was established during the Gold Rush.

### 1862
A new Grace Church opened at California and Stockton Streets.

### 1906
The earthquake caused relatively limited damage to the church,
but the subsequent fires destroyed the building.
