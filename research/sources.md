## Sources

### GC-001 — Grace Cathedral History

Institution:
Grace Cathedral

Type:
Institutional source

URL:
https://gracecathedral.org/history/

Subject:
History of Grace Church and Grace Cathedral

Key evidence:
- Grace's origins date to 1849.
- A larger brick Gothic church opened at California and
  Stockton Streets in 1862.
- The church burned during the 1906 San Francisco fire.
- The ruins remained visible for months.
- The congregation subsequently moved to the Crocker property
  at California and Taylor Streets.

Evidence status:
Verified by institutional source.

## Research Status

### Verified

- Grace Church existed at California and Stockton.
- The church opened in 1862.
- The church was destroyed in the 1906 fire.
- 600 Stockton became the Metropolitan Life Insurance Building.
- The Metropolitan Life building was constructed in 1909.
- 600 Stockton is San Francisco Landmark No. 167.

### Requires Verification

- Exact architect of Grace Church.
- Exact construction chronology.
- Original dimensions.
- Detailed architectural features.
- Exact relationship between Grace Church footprint
  and the later Metropolitan Life footprint.
- Exact chronology of the Metropolitan Life additions.

### Open Questions

- Who designed Grace Church?
- What did the original church look like from California Street?
- What did the interior look like?
- What buildings surrounded the church?
- Who owned the land before Grace Church?
- What happened to the property between 1906 and 1909?
- Why did Metropolitan Life choose this location?
- How did the 1906 disaster influence the new building's design?
- How did Nob Hill change socially after 1906?
